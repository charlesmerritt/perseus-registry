## Overview

A stand-alone project that takes estimates from the models and scenarios
generated by other Objective 2 Research Projects and makes them comparable,
in static and interactive views.

## Goals and milestones

- Apps in development to visualize model data; visualization tools ongoing
  (Jinha, 9/17).
- The framework has been used to ingest FIA and is expanding to Indiana and
  Georgia; it will be shared with the larger group shortly (Erin, 9/17).

## Needs from other Objectives

Model outputs from Tasks 2.1–2.3; Objective 3 stakeholder preferences and
decision-support needs.
