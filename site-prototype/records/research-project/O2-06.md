## Overview

Adapts the LANDIS-II forest landscape model to two new regions, Georgia and
north Florida (UGA) and the Central Hardwoods of Indiana (Purdue), so that
Objective 2 can simulate forest change under Objective 3's scenarios at
regional scale.

## Goals and milestones

  continues the project as a PhD student.

## Needs from other Objectives

- **Objective 1**: wall-to-wall forest data (via O2-16).
- **Objective 3**: climate, market, policy, pest, and management scenarios

## Methods

Calibration uses TreeMap for initial communities, repeated-measure FIA plots
for growth and mortality, regional species parameters, and improved
succession processes.
