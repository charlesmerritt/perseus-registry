## Overview

Creates static and interactive tools for comparing outputs across models and scenarios, including inventory, harvest, carbon, habitat, water, climate, market, and policy results.

This is a stand-alone project that takes estimates from the models and scenarios generated by other workstreams.

## Needs from other Objectives

Model outputs from Tasks 2.1-2.3; Objective 3 stakeholder preferences and decision-support needs.

## Integration notes

Confirmed as a stand-alone project that consumes estimates from multiple models/workstreams.
