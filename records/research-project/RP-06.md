## Overview

Adapts and calibrates LANDIS-II for Georgia/north Florida and Indiana/Central Hardwood forests using TreeMap, repeated-measure FIA data, species parameters, improved succession processes, and computational-performance enhancements.

## Needs from other Objectives

Objective 1 wall-to-wall forest data; Objective 3 climate, market, policy, pest, and management scenarios.

## Integration notes

Strong Objective 1 dependency for wall-to-wall forest data and Objective 3 dependency for scenarios.
