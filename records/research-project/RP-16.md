## Overview

Brings FIA, LiDAR, imagery, canopy-height, biomass, forest-structure, and other Objective 1 products into the Objective 2 data platform and model/visualization workflows. Maintains a Spatio-Temporal Asset Catalog (STAC) repository and automated ingestion processes that make large geospatial datasets easier to find and access. Provides a cloud-based environment for integrating, analyzing, visualizing, and sharing FIA, airborne LiDAR, imagery, and other Perseus geospatial data.

## Needs from other Objectives

Objective 1 source and derived data products, coverage, calibration, and product delivery; model outputs from Tasks 2.1-2.3; Objective 3 decision-support requirements.
